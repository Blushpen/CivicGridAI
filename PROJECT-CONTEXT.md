# CivicGrid AI Project Context

Project: CivicGrid AI - Smart Public Infrastructure & Complaint Resolution Platform

Core model: Gamified Civic Intelligence Platform
Tagline: Report. Earn. Compete. Improve your city.

## Problem
Citizens encounter infrastructure issues such as potholes, garbage accumulation, blocked drains, water leaks, flooding, damaged roads, and broken streetlights. Reporting and follow-up are often fragmented, and authorities face duplicate or unstructured issues that are harder to prioritize and resolve.

## Product goals
- Enable citizens to report issues with photos, text, and optional voice input.
- Use AI to classify, prioritize, and route issues to the proper department.
- Provide transparent status tracking for citizens, admins, and field workers.
- Reward verified civic participation via XP, reputation, badges, and leaderboards.

## MVP scope
The initial hackathon prototype must cover:
- citizen landing/dashboard
- issue reporting form
- evidence upload/preview
- AI classification result card
- duplicate/nearby warning
- map or map-like issue visualization
- admin issue queue
- assignment to worker/department
- worker status updates and resolution
- reputation, XP, badges, and leaderboard changes

## Constraints
- Focus on a stable 2-3 minute end-to-end demo.
- Do not expand scope beyond the core vertical slice.
- Keep the AI layer provider-agnostic with a deterministic fallback.
- Prefer free/open-source tools and avoid mandatory paid dependencies.
- Do not invent requirements that contradict the official LT HackFest brief or project decisions.

## Team model
- 4 developers / 4 feature areas
- VS Code as the primary editor
- GitHub as the single source of truth
- main remains the stable branch; feature branches are used for development
